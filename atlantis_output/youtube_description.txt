Did the ancient Egyptians know about Atlantis before Plato ever wrote about it?

Carved into the walls of the Temple of Edfu, the Edfu Building Texts describe a sacred island of the "Primeval Ones": gods who lived on a homeland surrounded by water, until a great flood destroyed it. The survivors are said to have sailed away and brought their knowledge to Egypt. Some researchers believe these texts preserve traditions far older than the temple itself, and older than Plato's account of Atlantis.
